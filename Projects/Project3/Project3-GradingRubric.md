# Project 3: Present Your Work

## Grading Rubric

This Project is meant to be an assessment of your ability to present the work from Project 1 and/or 2 in a poster-presentation format.    

### Title (5 points)

A title for the work that clearly describes the purpose of the project. 

  - Full credit: A descriptive title that is prominently displayed.
  - Half credit: A non-descriptive title that is non-prominently displayed. 
  - No credit: No title displayed.
  
### Authors (5 points)

Authors and their affiliations are listed in a prominent position. 

  - Full credit: Authors and affiliations are listed prominently. 
  - Half credit: Either affiliations are missing or both are not prominently displayed. 
  - No credit: No authors or affiliations listed on the poster. 
  
### Background (20 points)

The background section explains the necessary information needed to understand the poster and clearly stated question(s) that drives the visualizations.

  - Full credit: enough background is provided, either on the poster or verbally, that the viewer can understand the data set and questions. Questions should be prominently displayed. 
  - 20 points: Background is sufficient but the questions are missing or unclear. 
  - 15 points: Questions are clear and prominent but background is lacking. 
  - 10 points: Questions are absent and background is insufficient but some background is present. 
  - No credit: Questions are absent and background is grossly insufficient. 
  
### Analysis (10 points)

The analysis section that provides a reference to the data set and an abbreviated description of any analysis done for the visualizations.

  - Full credit: a short description of analysis is present. 
  - No credit: Analysis is absent. 

### Results (40 points)

The results section that provides the visualization(s) with captions and any other results.

  - Full credit: Visualizations are clear and centrally placed with adequate captions. 
  - 30 points: Visualizations are clear and centrally placed with inadequate captions. 
  - 20 points: Visualizations are smaller or have difficult to read guides, have mild problems with accessibility. 
  - 10 points: Visualizations are inadequate in a major way, either missing guides, inaccessible, or unreadable. Captions are inadequate or missing. 
  - No credit: no visualizations are present. 

### Conclusions (10 points)

The conclusions section that provides a list of conclusions derived from the visualizations that answer the question(s) in the Background.

  - Full credit: conclusions are clear in a bulleted or numbered list. 
  - 5 points: conclusions are too long or unclear but present. 
  - No credit: conclusions are too long or inadequate. 

### Acknowledgements and References (10 points) 

These sections thanks anyone who has helped you with the work that's not an author and reference works on which the project relies. This includes references to images not created by the poster's author. 

  - Full credit: Both acknowledgements and references are present and sufficient. 
  - 5 points: one section is missing, or a reference is missing. 
  - No credit: both sections are absent. 

### Generative AI statement 

If generative AI was used to create any part of the poster (including the code for the visualization).

  - No penalty: a statement is present and describes what aspects of the poster and project were created using AI. 
  - 25% penalty on final score: AI was used for any part of the poster or project but the statement was not present.
